# 🌿 UNWIND

> Breathe slower.  
> Live softer.  
> Heal gently.

Unwind is more than a self-care app.

It is a calming digital safe space designed to help people slow down, reflect, breathe, and reconnect with themselves through immersive wellness experiences.

---

#  THE EXPERIENCE

Unwind was designed to feel:

- calming
- emotionally warm
- modern
- immersive
- peaceful
- visually comforting

Instead of overwhelming users with productivity pressure, Unwind focuses on emotional balance, mindfulness, and small daily wins.

---

# FEATURES

### 🧘 Mindfulness Tools
Breathing exercises, calming sessions, and wellness activities.

### 😊 Mood Tracking
Track emotions daily and reflect on your feelings.

### 📖 Journal Space
A private area to write thoughts, gratitude, and reflections.

### 🌿 Daily Wellness
Healthy routines and gentle reminders.

### ✨ Immersive UI
Soft gradients, glassmorphism, calming animations, and modern mobile-inspired design.

---

#  DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Unwind combines:

- minimalism
- emotional design
- modern mobile aesthetics
- calming color psychology

to create a digital environment users genuinely enjoy returning to.
